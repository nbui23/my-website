## About me {#about}

lately i'm trying to distance myself from social media, so i've gotten into reading a lot more and i run crosspoint on an xteink x4 so i always have a dedicated e-reader in my pocket. my favourites so far have been murakami's <span class="g-hbw">hard-boiled wonderland and the end of the world</span> and sanderson's <span class="g-mistborn">mistborn</span>.

since moving to toronto i've also been watching a lot of shows and movies with my girlfriend (recently we've liked <span class="g-invite">the invite</span> and currently getting through <span class="g-silo">silo s3</span>).

i've been bouldering on and off for over four years and despite tearing my rotator cuff, a subluxed shoulder, and perpetually tweaky finger tendons, i keep coming back just to climb <span class="g-v4">v4</span>.

i completely stepped away from competitive fps after moving to toronto, but i spent a looong time near the top of the ladder: between the top 1% and top 0.1% in <span class="g-val">val</span>, <span class="g-ow"><span class="g-ow-o">o</span>w</span>, <span class="g-cs">cs</span>, and <span class="g-kvks">kvks</span>.

i get pretty obsessive about whatever i'm into at the time and usually stick with it for a long time until i get good at it, which is more or less how i got into software too.
